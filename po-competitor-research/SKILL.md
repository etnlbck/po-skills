# Competitor research

## When

- Running **monthly** or ad hoc **competitive** analysis for a product area.
- Standardizing **what to capture** and how to compare.

## Outcomes

- Repeatable research output using a shared framework; easier to diff month-over-month.

## Framework (distilled)

### Cadence and scope

- **Cadence:** Typically monthly (e.g. first week for prior month).
- **Products:** **SIT** (inventory discovery, VLP/VDP, path to dealer) and **SST** (service scheduling).
- **Brands:** **Lexus** (luxury) and **Toyota** (volume)—each with its own competitor set.

### Competitor sets (default examples)

| Segment | Luxury (Lexus) | Volume (Toyota) |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Default** | Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Genesis, Acura | Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Hyundai, Nissan |

Review **3–5** competitors per brand per month; refresh the list periodically.

### Dimensions — SIT

| Dimension | What to note |
|-----------|--------------|
| **MSP / model entry** | How users pick model; trim/grade clarity. |
| **VLP (listing)** | Filters, sort, density, mobile, pagination. |
| **VDP (details)** | Specs, imagery, availability, CTAs (dealer, test drive, digital retail). |
| **FIFM / “find for me”** | Presence and flow. |
| **Path to dealer / purchase** | Dealer referral, lead forms, digital retail. |

### Dimensions — SST

| Dimension | What to note |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Entry / vehicle** | Vehicle selection; VIN/account linkage. |
| **Date and time** | Calendar, slots, advisor. |
| **Dealer** | Home/preferred dealer, search, map. |
| **Services** | Factory vs dealer recommended, pricing, notes. |
| **Review and confirm** | Summary, edit, confirmation. |

### Metrics context

Read competitor findings alongside **your** leading and lagging metrics (**[po-metrics-leading-lagging](../po-metrics-leading-lagging/SKILL.md)**). You rarely have competitors’ internal numbers; use qualitative flow comparison to infer **drivers** of your metrics and narrative (“why” your lagging metrics move).

### Monthly process

1. Open last month’s artifact for “what changed.”
2. Summarize **your** latest SIT/SST metrics (short).
3. Walk competitor flows (desktop + mobile if feasible).
4. Fill dimensions; capture strength/weakness vs Toyota/Lexus and **change vs last month**.
5. Write **summary and implications** (2–3 bullets for SIT, 2–3 for SST; optional Lexus vs Toyota split).
6. Optional: **Implications for leading/lagging metrics** (1–2 bullets).
7. Save as `YYYY-MM-competitor-research.md` in your team’s docs folder.
