# PO coverage maps

## When

- Documenting **who covers which product or program** (e.g. Toyota SIT vs SST) for stakeholders or onboarding.
- Aligning **Confluence** or internal wiki with **Jira** ownership language.

## Outcomes

- A single table or page that maps **products**, **surfaces**, and **PO / liaison** roles without duplicating full org charts.

## What to include

| Column / section | Purpose |
|------------------|---------|
| **Product or program** | e.g. SIT, SST, cross-cutting initiative |
| **Surfaces or scope** | Key user-facing areas or journeys |
| **Primary PO / owner** | DRI for decisions and backlog alignment |
| **Liaison / secondary** | Optional: backup, cross-team contact |
| **Notes** | Space keys, boards, or caveats |

Keep the map **lightweight**—link out to detailed org charts rather than copying them.
