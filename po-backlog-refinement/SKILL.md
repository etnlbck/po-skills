# Backlog and refinement

## When

- Preparing or running **backlog refinement** for SIT (or similar) with a consistent agenda.
- You need a **repeatable outline** for timeboxed discussion.

## Outcomes

- A published or shared agenda; teams know what to prep and what “ready” means for the next sprint.

## Agenda skeleton (distilled)

Structure the session so discussion stays focused:

1. **Priority tickets** — Review top items first; confirm scope, owners, and dependencies.
2. **Other tickets (if time)** — Deeper dives or estimates.
3. **Intake** — New items, quick triage, defer or convert to spikes.
4. **Follow-ups / open questions / horizon** — Parking lot themes that are not blocking this sprint.
5. **Parking lot** — Explicitly deferred topics.
6. **Action items** — Who does what before the next ceremony.

Link to **team priorities** or program context where your org maintains it (wiki, Confluence, etc.).

Ensure items entering refinement meet **DoR** expectations per **[po-ticketing](../po-ticketing/SKILL.md)**.
