# Clarity and ticket framing

## When

- A ticket or initiative feels **vague**, or stakeholders disagree on scope.
- You want a **short, repeatable** structure before writing long ACs.

## Outcomes

- Every item has an explicit **user problem**, **desired outcome**, and **constraints** so engineering and design can execute without guesswork.

## 3-Line Clarity Block

Use this for every ticket so nothing is vague.

1. **User problem** — What uncertainty or pain does the user have?
2. **Desired outcome** — What does “done” look like for the user?
3. **Constraints** — What is fixed (scope, time, quality bar)?
