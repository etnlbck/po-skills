# Vision, strategy, and metrics

## When

- Defining or refreshing **product narrative** for SIT, SST, or AI-innovation themes.
- Connecting **company vision** → **goals** → **initiatives** → **metrics**, including **T1** or yearly planning.

## Outcomes

- A documented hierarchy stakeholders can cite; metrics trace to product **surfaces** (e.g. MSP, VLP, VDP, SST steps).

## Framework (distilled)

1. **Vision** — North-star narrative for the product or program.
2. **Strategy** — Bets and focus areas that realize the vision.
3. **Initiatives** — Named efforts (often epics or programs) with owners and time horizons.
4. **Metrics** — Leading and lagging indicators tied to surfaces and funnel steps (see **[po-metrics-leading-lagging](../po-metrics-leading-lagging/SKILL.md)**).

For **SIT** (Search Inventory Tool), tie narrative to discovery and path-to-action surfaces (e.g. model entry, VLP, VDP, path to dealer). For **SST** (Service Scheduling Tool), tie to scheduling steps (vehicle → date/time → dealer → services → confirm).

**T1 / AI laddering** — When relevant, show how AI or innovation work ladders from vision down to measurable outcomes and guardrails.
