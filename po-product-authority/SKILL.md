# Product authority and growth (N8LP-style)

## When

- Working on **personal product authority**, phased growth, or structured **OKRs** alongside delivery work.
- Running a **Confusion Audit** or mapping journeys on a **Certainty Ladder**.

## Outcomes

- Confluence-ready (or draft) pages that align **Phase 1–3** style initiatives with measurable OKRs and reusable narrative templates.

## Framework (distilled)

### Phases (conceptual)

- **Phase 1 — Stabilization and authority** — Baseline clarity, reliable delivery, credibility with stakeholders.
- **Phase 2 — Strategic expansion** — Broader bets once the foundation holds.
- **Phase 3 — Leadership scaling** — Influence, multi-team alignment, coaching others.

### Artifacts to produce

- **Vision & strategy** — Short narrative linking authority goals to product strategy.
- **OKRs** — Objectives with measurable key results; tie to leading/lagging metrics where possible.
- **Confusion Audit** — Where users or stakeholders experience ambiguity; prioritized fixes.
- **Certainty Ladder** — Levels of clarity from “unknown” to “validated” for critical decisions or journeys.

Keep pages **link-heavy and concise**—avoid pasting full template bodies; reference your team’s canonical Confluence or wiki when publishing.
